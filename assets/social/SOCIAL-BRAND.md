# CLocal social branding (match the website)

Use the same vibe as [clocal.co.uk](https://clocal.co.uk): soft cream, teal, sage, friendly, local, rewards.

## Colours

| Name | Hex | Use |
|------|-----|-----|
| Vibrant Teal | `#008080` | Logo, buttons, main text accents |
| Bright Sage | `#77DD77` | Soft badges, highlights |
| Intense Turquoise | `#00FFFF` | Light glow only (don’t flood posts) |
| Cream | `#F4FBF8` | Backgrounds |
| Ink | `#1A2E2E` | Body text |

Avoid: purple, heavy black, neon-only posts, stocky corporate blue.

## Fonts (same as site)

- Headlines / body: **Nunito** (bold, friendly)
- Fun lines: **Caveat** (handwriting feel)

## Voice (how to write)

Sound like the website:
- Short. Warm. Local.
- Good lines to reuse:
  - Never miss what’s local.
  - Love where you live.
  - Small business is a Big deal.
  - You can’t buy happiness but you can CLocal.
  - Buy local. Support local.
  - Every visit puts money back into your neighbourhood.

Don’t sound like:
- Hard creator hustle / paid-promo spam
- “Content creator grind”
- Cold directory / Yellow Pages clone

## Photo vibe

High-end local lifestyle — elegant, Gen Z creator chic.
Think: boutiques, mini designer bags, salon beauty, coffee, nights out, dining.
Polished and elevated. Avoid hippie / rustic / sporty-studio clichés.
