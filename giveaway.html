Giveaway Rules

Brunch at General Merchants · Last updated 15 October 2026

This giveaway is promoted by several independent Instagram pages and by CLocal. However you came across it, and whichever page you saw it on, there is one prize draw and every entry goes into the same hat. No page has its own separate draw and no page's followers get a better chance than anyone else's.

How to enter

Join the CLocal waitlist at clocal.co.uk before the closing time below, and follow @clocal_app on Instagram. One entry per person. Entry is free and there is nothing to buy.

We check the follow when we contact a winner, not when you enter, so joining the waitlist is what puts you in the hat. If a drawn winner is not following us at that point we will say so and give them a chance to put it right before we draw again.

Extra entries. Anyone already on the waitlist can share their own link. For every friend who joins through that link, the person who shared it gets one more entry in the draw, up to five extra entries. Extra entries only ever add chances: nobody who has already entered loses anything, and you do not have to share anything to be in the draw. An entry is only added once the friend has joined with a working email address, and entries added for addresses that bounce or are obviously made up are removed before the draw.

Closing time

Entries close at 11:59pm on Friday 31 October 2026. Anything received after that does not go into the draw.

The prizes

First prize: a year of CLocal VIP free, and a brunch voucher for General Merchants. One person wins both of these together.

Two runner-up prizes: a brunch voucher for General Merchants each. So three people in all win a voucher, and one of those three also wins the VIP year.

About the vouchers. They are bought by CLocal and given as they come; they are not transferable and there is no cash alternative. General Merchants is the venue the prize is spent at and nothing more: they do not run this giveaway, they have not endorsed it, and they are not responsible for it. Voucher expiry and any conditions printed on the voucher apply.

About the VIP year. VIP normally costs £11.99 a month. The year is added to the winner's own CLocal account, so they need to have one: if the app is not out yet when they win, the year starts from the day we add it to their account rather than the day of the draw, and they lose nothing by waiting. It is not transferable and there is no cash alternative.

Who can enter

You must be 18 or over and live in the United Kingdom or Ireland. Employees of Clickclick Video Marketing Ltd and their immediate families cannot enter, and neither can anyone at a page promoting the giveaway.

Picking the winners

Three names are drawn at random from all valid entries within five working days of the closing time, and contacted using the email address they joined the waitlist with. The first name drawn takes first prize, the VIP year and a voucher. The next two each take a voucher. Nobody is drawn twice. If we cannot reach a winner within seven days of writing to them, we draw that prize again.

Nothing to do with Instagram

This giveaway is in no way sponsored, endorsed, administered by, or associated with Instagram or Meta. You are providing your information to CLocal and not to Instagram. Instagram is completely released from any responsibility by everyone who enters.

Your details

Entering puts you on the CLocal waitlist, which means we email you about the app launching. You can unsubscribe at any time using the link in any email, and unsubscribing does not remove you from the draw. We do not sell your details or pass them to the pages promoting the giveaway. See our Privacy Policy.

The rest

We may cancel or change this giveaway if something outside our control makes it unworkable, and we will say so on this page if that happens. Entering means you accept these rules. The law of Northern Ireland applies.
