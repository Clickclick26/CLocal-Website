Two shops already on CLocal · South and East Belfast

CLocal is a video-first app for supporting the local businesses near you, and getting rewarded for it. A shop posts a short video of what it has on that week, and people who go get something back for it.

Here are two real ones. We made both of these for the shop, at no cost to them.

What you get

  • A page with your name, your photos and what you have on.
  • Short videos like the two above. We make the first one for you.
  • Offers you can put up and take down yourself, whenever you want.
  • People nearby see it when they are close enough to walk in.

What it costs

Nothing to list, and nothing to pay for the first video. We are opening in South and East Belfast first, so the shops that come in now are the ones people see.

How to take your page over

There is already a page for you. Your email has a link in it that makes the page yours, and it only works once. Open it, sign in, and the page is in your hands.

If you would rather the page came down, reply to that email and it comes down the same day.
